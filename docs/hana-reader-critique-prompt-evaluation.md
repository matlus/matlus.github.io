# Reader-critique additions to the publication review

Baseline: `eafb2fb`. The owner supplied Hana's Reader-Critique Prompt and
authorized selective article revisions through separate checked and merged PRs.
The prompt is a pedagogical review, and much of it already exists in
`review-article-teaching-and-evidence.md`. These additions make the review state
the learning outcome and article form, name a strong and any muddy explanation,
and identify teaching moves that a revision should preserve. Density checks now
also consider raw data, repeated JSON and conceptual continuity through code.
Series observations remain separate from article corrections.

This extends the existing fresh teaching/evidence review. It adds no extra
mandatory model session, fixed article outline or demand to rewrite a reference
as an essay. Findings still require passages, evidence and independent
adjudication. The existing comparison, rendered inspection, metadata extraction,
asset preservation, audits and publication checks continue to apply.

## Limited trial

The trial used five non-archival articles at the baseline, with candidate copies
outside the public content collection. Initial reviewers received complete texts,
voice guidance and the revised prompt without Hana's critiques. Two reviewers
disclosed accidental exposure to earlier conclusions during broad source
discovery; those passes were excluded and replaced with restricted clean packets.
An independent fresh comparison read all five complete pairs before reading
the findings and dispositions.

| Article | Form and trial change | Comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expense Analysis, Part One | Prompt comparison and replay; label ungraded output, explain missing original input evidence and close the largest-change check. | Improved. |
| Expense Analysis, Part Two | Python walkthrough; orient the reader, pin the illustrated source and define routing/ledger responsibilities directly. | Improved. |
| Logs and Artifacts: Pipeline Breadcrumbs | Library design lesson; distinguish the concept path from implementation detail and lead with live-run evidence. | Improved. |
| Jev: A Practical Reference | Lookup reference; state documentary scope, illustrate score uncertainty and explain agreement including review decisions. | Improved. |
| Expense Analysis, Part Three | Unchanged comparison control; its separate coverage and evidence-location findings remain for the article's issue pass. | Equivalent; unchanged. |

The initial reviews found useful concerns beyond Hana's suggestions: Part Two's
mutable source link, Part One's unfinished largest-change check, and the
reference's agreement denominator. They also supported preserving the reference
catalog, concrete examples and firm author positions. The complete expense log
and recorded output in Part One were already collapsed, so that criticism
required no layout change. New production experience, cleaner model results,
unmeasured economic gains and terminology etymology cannot be invented to make
an article more persuasive.

The Breadcrumbs request for a small verified diagnostic trace remains for its
article pass; the trial adds no invented recorded evidence. Part Three remains
unchanged in this trial even though the initial review found useful concerns.
An unchanged comparison verdict establishes preservation, not that the baseline
has no problems. This was a selective editorial pilot, not a seeded-defect
benchmark, and it establishes no detection rate or guarantee against later
criticism. Detailed packets, reviews, dispositions, candidate copies and logs
are retained outside the repository under the owner's local Codex documents.

No public article, original blog source, historical publication copy, chapter,
tested code, recorded output or artwork changes in this workflow PR. The
workflow additions are committed separately from subsequent article revisions.
