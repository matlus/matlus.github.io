# Publication date audit

Reviewed on 2026-09-25 for issue #10. The inventory covers all 20 writing posts and
12 PWI corpus chapters, including every entry on the Method Design tag page.

## Date basis

Historical PWI guideline material without a direct video adaptation uses the
existing corpus origin, 2017-09-20. Clean Abstractions Around Libraries and
Intentional Model Design develop those guidelines; their recent page creation
now appears as the 2026-09-25 update date. The four contemporary AI articles retain
their existing dates because their subjects are current model workflows and AI
verification, even when they use programming guidelines as examples.

The public [Programming With Intent - Method Design Part 1 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-13KJSZuR9k)
was published on 2017-09-20. Its description lists Method Design at 2:33 and
Naming is Everything at 9:17. The method-design and naming corpus chapters already
carry that day. Their current editions contain later examples and refinements,
which retain their actual update dates.

All 14 primary source videos and both supplementary recordings were checked
against public YouTube watch-page metadata on the review date. Their titles and
channel IDs matched the linked videos on Shiv's channel
`UC1PhnlVt1hE1UrmOsZwblig`. Publication dates use YouTube's calendar day. Two
existing article dates were a day later than the video's displayed day:

| Article | Previous date | YouTube displayed date | YouTube publication timestamp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Async-Await in C# | 2012-10-01 | 2012-09-30 | 2012-09-30T21:47:45-07:00 |
| C# 8 - Async Streams | 2020-01-20 | 2020-01-19 | 2020-01-19T16:25:43-08:00 |

Both article dates now use the displayed day. Converting those timestamps to UTC
would move them to the following day. That timezone conversion is inappropriate
for the site's calendar-date fields.

Factory Method continues to use its primary C# demonstration's 2013-02-10 date.
The supplementary discussion was published on 2019-07-29. Async-Await Part 2 was
published on 2012-10-06; Part 1 supplies the article's date. These later recordings
do not change the primary source's date.
