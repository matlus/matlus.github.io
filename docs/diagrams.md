article use. Generate related diagrams with

reads the article or supplied source and writes the complete drawing brief.

The functional acceptance illustration is the approved visual reference. Keep
its moderate heading scale, pastel palette, outlined dimensional platforms, and
curved ribbon arrows when those connections serve the new subject.

The CPU illustration preserves the supplied slide's four-core arrangement,
cache counts and sizes, shared layers, side blocks, and numeric annotations.
Its values reproduce that particular example.

Both images were generated using the built-in image generation tool. The JPEGs
retain their native dimensions with no artificial enlargement. Their PNG originals
remain in the generating chat's image archive outside Git.

When integrating an illustration into an article, provide descriptive alt text
and a caption or nearby explanation. Verify its labels and relationships against
the article. Coordinate replacements with the article's editorial work.
