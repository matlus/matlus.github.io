# Functional acceptance-testing publication

The sixteen articles use June 15, 2026 as their publication date and September 27,
2026 as their revision date. The reading order lives in
`src/data/acceptance-series.ts` and appears on `/acceptance-testing/`.

Each article has its own panoramic vintage ink hero and an adjacent generation
prompt in `src/assets/heroes`. Ten new topic heroes reuse the corresponding
article image, with provenance recorded in their prompt files.

Informative diagrams are authored inline SVG with readable text, accessible
titles and descriptions, and site design tokens. Diagram markers allow Markdown
exports to retain the explanatory caption while omitting the SVG markup.

Meridian Ordering remains a private training reference project. The published
pages include inline examples and an explicit source-access qualification.
The supporting checkout, source archive, local evidence files, and private
repository links are excluded from publication. Public source cross-links can
be added when the owner makes the repositories public.

The execution results described in the articles are the recorded local Meridian
receipts: 77 selected ordering/composer cases and 18 assertion-report cases.
Website publication does not claim another Meridian execution, a cloud-provider
run, or an executed mutation/refactoring experiment.
